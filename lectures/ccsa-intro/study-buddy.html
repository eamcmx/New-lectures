Study Buddy — a tutor in the page

PAAL · CourseForge session kit

A Socratic tutor that lives right here, knows this lecture inside-out, and never just hands you the answer. It runs on your own free Mistral API key — stored only in your browser, sent only to Mistral. Paste a key once and start asking.

Step 0 · Get your free key (2 minutes)

  1. Go to console.mistral.ai and create a free account (the free tier is plenty for studying).
  2. In the console, open API Keys and click Create new key.
  3. Copy the key and paste it below, then hit Save key. That's it — the chat unlocks instantly.
A key is saved in this browser.

Honest privacy note: your key is stored in this browser's localStorage under ccsa-mistral-key and is sent to exactly one place: api.mistral.ai, when you send a message. It never touches any other server — this page has no backend. Avoid saving it on a shared or lab computer, and use "Forget key" when you're done there.

Why it won't just tell you

The buddy is deliberately Socratic. When it answers a question with a question, that's not evasion — retrieving and reasoning your way to an answer is what builds the skill. Notice how a good hint feels annoying for five seconds and satisfying for a week.
