Retrieval beats rereading

Notice the little effort right before you flip — that reach into memory is where learning actually happens. Rereading the lecture feels smooth because recognition is easy; recalling from a blank card what made the Z3 different from the ABC is hard, and that difficulty is precisely what writes the fact in. Always guess before you peek.

The boxes are honest

A card you've "got" twice still comes back, just more slowly — box 2 every other session, box 3 every fourth. That's not the system distrusting you; it's the system knowing that forgetting is the default. Each return visit, spaced a bit wider, is what turns "ENIAC, 1945, 17,468 tubes" from a phrase you recognize into one you own.
