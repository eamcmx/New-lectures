How to use this set

Each page pairs with a section of the lecture slides. Nothing here is decoration: every slider and draggable dot is a claim from the slides that you can test. A good rhythm — read the slide, predict what the interactive will do, then check. When the picture and the formula agree in your head, you own the concept.
